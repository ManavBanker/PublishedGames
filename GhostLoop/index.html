Guide energy sparks to their destinations across sequential time phases. Avoid temporal paradoxes caused by intersecting past paths!

SELECT LEVEL
HOW TO PLAY

1. Record Path: Draw a route from the active Start Terminal to End Terminal by dragging grid cells, clicking adjacent tiles, or using WASD/Arrow keys.


2. Run Phase: Once at the End Terminal, hit RUN PHASE to lock in your path and trigger the time-loop simulation.


3. Avoid Paradoxes: During simulation, previously locked paths play simultaneously. Sparks MUST NOT occupy the same tile or swap adjacent tiles at the exact same tick!


4. Complete Loop: Complete all 4 phases (Alpha, Beta, Gamma, Delta) to solve the level.
