How to Play

• You control the BLUE (Real) Node directly using WASD, Arrows, or the virtual D-Pad.

• The PINK (Reflection) Node moves simultaneously based on the current Symmetry Rule.

• The active Symmetry Rule shifts after set intervals! Watch the SHIFT IN indicator carefully.

• Navigate obstacles. Use walls strategically to get nodes in and out of sync.

• Land both nodes on their respective glowing Cyan & Pink Symmetry Gates on the exact same move to win.

• Touch a Red Void Hazard or exceed par, and the level resets. Use Undo (Z) or Double Tap to experiment safely!
