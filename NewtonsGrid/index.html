How to Play

Navigate your Gold Spherical Probe to the Indigo Core Portal.

The Dynamic Grid Mechanic:
- Moving UP shifts your vacated column DOWN.
- Moving DOWN shifts your vacated column UP.
- Moving LEFT shifts your vacated row RIGHT.
- Moving RIGHT shifts your vacated row LEFT.

Avoid falling into the Crimson Voids. Push Terracotta Crates to block pathways or sacrifice them into voids to clear a safe path.
