How to Play

Goal: Slide the block into the golden exit with a value of exactly 0.

Movement: Use Keyboard Arrow Keys, WASD, or swipe to fling across frictionless ice until hitting a Wall.

Operators: Passing over math pads changes your value instantly.

Limit: Keep values between 0 and 99. Going out of limits triggers instant failure!
