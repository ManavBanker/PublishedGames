How to Play

Goal: Slide the block into the golden exit with a value of exactly 0.

Movement: Use Keyboard Arrow Keys, WASD, or swipe the screen to fling. Because of the frictionless ice, you slide until hitting a Wall or Edge.

Operators: Passing over math pads changes your value instantly.

Limit: Keeping values between 0 and 99. Going out of limits triggers instant drift failure!

Zoom & Pan Tools: Toggle 🖐 to dynamic pan, zoom using virtual control triggers or hold keyboard controls. Two-finger touch scales elements natively!
