How to Play

Objective: Enclose the glowing Essence Nodes completely inside your loop, then press Space or click CONSTRICT to digest them!

Movement: Use WASD, Arrow Keys, or swipe to roll your elastic loop along the grid.

Elasticity: Squeezing through narrow walls forces your loop to warp and stretch. Be careful not to let the loop snap!

Snapping: Moving into walls or crossing your own loop will cause it to snap instantly.

Parasites: Moving patrols. If they touch your outer boundary you snap. Capture them inside and constrict for massive bonus points!

Zoom & Pan: Drag screen with Hand Tool or pinch-to-zoom to see different regions. Use Ctrl + and Ctrl - on keyboards.
