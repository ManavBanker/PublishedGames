Quantum Instructions

Solve asymmetrical levels using mirrored physics.


• You control the Blue Node. The Pink Node mirrors its vertical axis (Left moves Right, Up moves Up).

• To desynchronize their positions, crash one node into a block or obstacle.

• Land both on their corresponding goals on the exact same turn to solve.


Tile Mechanics:

• One-Way Arrows: Can only step onto them in the direction of the arrow.

• Swap Gates (G/g): Switch state (open/solid) on every step taken.

• Singularities (H): Instantly collapses state if touched.
