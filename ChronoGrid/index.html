CHRONO-GRID is a turn-based temporal puzzle game. Your goal is to collect all energy cores and reach the Exit Portal.

The Loop: Collecting an energy core instantly resets the level, but a Ghost of your previous run is spawned, repeating your exact movements.

Cooperate: Use your Ghosts to stand on pressure plates to open locked gates or block deadly laser beams from vaporizing you.

The Paradox: Touching a ghost or occupying their slot at the same time causes an instant timeline collapse!

Pinch & Drag Pan: Use two fingers to pinch zoom and drag pan on the main canvas screen anytime (even in pause mode). Use keyboard Ctrl + + / - keys or the virtual Zoom tools below!

Controls: Arrow keys/WASD to move, Space/Enter/Tap Center to wait a turn.
